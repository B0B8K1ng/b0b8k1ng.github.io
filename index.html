03 / THE METHOD

From seeing motion
to understanding it.

A shared latent-action space connects broad visual experience with executable navigation actions.

The latent action model is trained first. World-model training then proceeds through latent pretraining, physical-action adapter warmup, and joint post-training. At inference, planning optimizes real waypoint actions.
