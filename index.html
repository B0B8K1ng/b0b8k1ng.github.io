How this demo works

Preset videos are precomputed OpenNWM outputs for their displayed trajectories. Drawing a custom path requires the live GPU service; a preset video is never substituted for a custom prediction. Paths describe movement on a flat plane, not collision-free plans or drone altitude.

05 / THE METHOD

From seeing motion
to understanding it.

A shared latent-action space connects broad visual experience with executable navigation actions.

The latent action model is trained first. World-model training then proceeds through latent pretraining, physical-action adapter warmup, and joint post-training. At inference, planning optimizes real waypoint actions.
